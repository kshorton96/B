Preserving our shared history

About the Historical Society & this atlas

This atlas is being developed for the Harmon County Historical Society as a place to preserve and explore the county’s history. Researched entries will bring together historical places, people, photographs, documents and source references.

The initial collection contains illustrative examples, not authenticated historical entries. Each future record needs evidence, location certainty and publication permission.

Every recollection has a place

A family photograph, a remembered schoolhouse, a name on the back of a picture — your knowledge can help preserve the county’s story.
