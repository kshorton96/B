About this atlas

A starter project for recording historic places, people, photographs and documents across Harmon County. Records should include sources and location certainty. The included sample entries are illustrative and must be researched before publication.

Built with Leaflet and OpenStreetMap. Map tiles require an internet connection. © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Share your history

Share a Photograph or Story or help identify Harmon County history. No account is needed to prepare a contribution. All material needs museum review and separate publication permission.
